# Sharma Textiles - Owner Dashboard Requirements
# Location: src/app/demo/owner/

## Core Features
1. CEO Briefing Widget: Summarizes cash balances, pipeline orders, dyeing defect patterns, and review count.
2. Decision Desk: Handles Voice-directed AI capture simulation with pulse recording, thinking animations, text directive inputs, and pending approval cards.
3. Company Brain Ask AI: Interactive mock-scan of documents with 3 suggested queries.
4. Finance Overview: Monthly bar chart with 6-month historical overview.
5. Directory List: Team member avatars and profiles.

## Styling Rules
- Neobrutalist high-contrast inputs.
- Active brand-red accent borders.
