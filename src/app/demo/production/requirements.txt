# Sharma Textiles - Production Dashboard Requirements
# Location: src/app/demo/production/

## Core Features
1. Fulfilment Pipeline Board: A 5-stage production tracking board (Preparation, Loom Setup, Dyeing, Packing, Dispatch).
2. Advanced Stages: Advance jobs through stages or mark them as "Hold" to halt production issues.
3. Operations Checklist: Floor list tracking warp tension and coloring batch priorities.
4. Alerts Window: Warning notifications for dyeing stocks and loom friction.

## Styling Rules
- Active brand-green border accents.
- Brutalist card borders and status pill colors.
